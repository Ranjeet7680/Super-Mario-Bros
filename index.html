Settings & Accessibility

🎮 PlayStation 5 (DualSense) Controls

  • Move / Crouch: Left Stick or D-Pad (◀ / ▶ / ▼)

  • Dash Attack / Stomp: ▢ (Square) or R1 / Circle
  • Speak / Read Lore: △ (Triangle)

  • Run: A / D or Left / Right Arrows (or Virtual ◀ / ▶)
  • Jump: W or Space (or Virtual ⬆️)
  • Dash: Shift or K or X (or Virtual ⚡)
  • Fast Fall: S or Down Arrow (or Virtual ▼)
  • Talk: E or F (or Virtual 🗣️)
